# Leave policy

This policy applies to all permanent employees of Brightline Logistics. Contractors follow the leave terms in their contract.

## Annual leave

Employees get 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Leave accrues monthly, so a new joiner earns 1.83 days for each full month worked.

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the first quarter of the next year. Carried-over days that are not taken by 31 March are lost.

Requests for more than 5 consecutive days must be approved by your manager at least 2 weeks in advance.

## Sick leave

Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry over.

Tell your manager before your shift starts, or as early as possible. A doctor's certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver gets 26 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver gets 4 weeks of paid parental leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## Bereavement leave

Employees get 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member, and 2 days for other relatives.
