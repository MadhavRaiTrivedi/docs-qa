# Expense policy

Brightline Logistics reimburses reasonable costs that employees pay themselves while doing company work.

## Submitting a claim

Submit every claim in the Expenses app within 30 days of the purchase. Attach an itemised receipt for any expense above 500 rupees. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the travel desk. Economy class is the default for flights shorter than 6 hours.

Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed when public transport is not practical, for example late at night or with heavy equipment. Write the business reason on the claim.

## Meals

When travelling for work, meals are reimbursed up to 1,500 rupees per day in metro cities and 1,000 rupees per day elsewhere. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Equipment

Home office equipment such as a chair or monitor needs pre-approval from your manager. The limit is 15,000 rupees per employee every 3 years.
