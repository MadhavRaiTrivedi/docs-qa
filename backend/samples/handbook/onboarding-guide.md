# Onboarding guide

Welcome to Brightline Logistics. This guide covers your first two weeks.

## Before your first day

You will receive your laptop by courier 2 days before you start. Do not set it up until IT sends the activation email on your first morning.

## First day

Your manager meets you at 10:00 for a welcome session. In the afternoon you complete the security training, which is mandatory before you get access to production systems.

## Accounts and access

Request access to tools through the IT portal. Access to customer data needs approval from your manager and the data protection officer, and is reviewed every 6 months.

## Probation

The probation period is 6 months. Your manager holds a check-in at the end of month 1 and month 3, and a review at the end of probation. During probation, the notice period is 2 weeks; after probation it is 2 months.

## Buddy programme

Every new joiner gets a buddy from another team for the first 3 months. Your buddy is your first contact for questions about how things work here.
